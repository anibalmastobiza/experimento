Study Information

In this study you will read 35 short moral scenarios and judge whether the described action is morally permissible or impermissible. After each judgment you will rate your confidence. On some trials you will also be asked to briefly explain your reasoning.

There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in your genuine moral intuitions. The study takes approximately 20–25 minutes.

Your responses are anonymous. Data will be used exclusively for academic research and stored securely.
